Jain Group of Institutions Accounts Officer Jain Heritage School Whitefield

Salary : Based on Experience
Full-time On-Site Finance Accounting
Job Description

We are looking for a skilled Accounts Officer to perform a variety of accounting, bookkeeping, and financial tasks. The ideal candidate will be responsible for processing business transactions, preparing financial statements, and ensuring all accounting activities comply with legal requirements and company policies.

Responsibilities
  • Process and manage all accounts payable and receivable transactions.
  • Prepare and submit weekly/monthly financial reports and summaries.
  • Reconcile bank and credit card statements and resolve discrepancies.
  • Assist with budget preparation and variance analysis.
  • Manage company payroll and ensure timely disbursement.
  • Ensure all financial records are organized, up-to-date, and audit-ready.
  • Assist the management in financial planning and analysis.
  • Working under strict deadlines for monthly and quarterly closing procedures.
Educational Requirements
  • Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, or Commerce (B.Com/B.A. in Finance).
  • Professional certification (e.g., CA/CPA inter) or an MBA in Finance is a strong asset.
  • For people who have at least 5 years of proven, verifiable experience in accounting, educational qualifications may be compromised.
Skill & Experience
  • You have at least 3 years’ experience as an Accounts Officer or Junior Accountant.
  • Proficiency in accounting software (e.g., Tally, QuickBooks, SAP) and MS Excel.
  • Strong knowledge of Generally Accepted Accounting Principles (GAAP).
  • Quick learner and smart enough to learn new regulations and independent reporting standards.
  • Strong knowledge of industry standards and best practices for financial reporting.
