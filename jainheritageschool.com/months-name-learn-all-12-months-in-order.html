Months Name: Learn All 12 Months in Order with Easy Tricks

Understanding the months name is a basic but essential part of learning for every student. There are 12 months in a year, each with its own number of days, special events, and seasonal changes. In this blog, we explain each month in order and share fun tricks to remember them easily. Whether your child is learning at home or in a preschool in Whitefield, memorizing the 12 months name in English becomes simple with engaging methods.

What Are the 12 Months of the Year?

The months name in order are:

January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November, December

These names are used worldwide as part of the Gregorian calendar. A full months name chart helps students remember the sequence and spelling easily.

History: How Did the Months Get Their Names?

Most of the months name come from Latin and Roman culture:

  • January: Named after Janus, god of beginnings
  • February: From “Februa,” a purification festival
  • March: After Mars, god of war
  • April: From “aperire,” meaning “to open”
  • May: Named after Maia, goddess of growth
  • June: After Juno, goddess of marriage
  • July: Named for Julius Caesar
  • August: Named for Augustus Caesar
  • September–December: Based on Latin numbers 7–10

Fun Tricks: How to Remember the Months

One of the most popular methods is the knuckle trick:

  • Make a fist
  • Each knuckle = 31 days
  • Each space = 30 days (or February with 28/29 days)

Another easy rhyme:
“Thirty days hath September, April, June, and November...”

This simple rhyme helps kids memorize the 12 months name in English quickly.

Months Grouped by Seasons in India

  • Winter: December, January, February
  • Spring: March, April
  • Summer: May, June
  • Monsoon: July, August, September
  • Autumn: October, November

Festivals in Different Months

  • January: Republic Day, Pongal
  • March/April: Holi, Ram Navami
  • May: Buddha Purnima
  • August: Raksha Bandhan, Independence Day
  • October: Dussehra
  • November: Diwali
  • December: Christmas

Why Do We Need to Learn Months?

Knowing the months name in order helps in reading calendars, writing dates, planning events, and understanding seasons. It’s one of the first steps in early learning for kids.

Worksheets and Learning Activities

  • Fill blank calendars
  • Match months with festivals
  • Flashcards with months name
  • Arrange shuffled months in sequence

Bonus: Leap Year

February normally has 28 days, but in a leap year it has 29 days. Leap years occur every 4 years. For example, 2024 was a leap year.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Mixing month order
  • Forgetting which months have 30 or 31 days
  • Spelling mistakes like “Febuary”

Conclusion

Now that you know the months name, their order, history, and memory tricks, you can use this knowledge every day. Whether checking a calendar or planning celebrations, remembering the months helps students stay organized and confident. Teachers can also use these methods to make learning fun and engaging.

FAQs

1. How many months are there in a year?
There are 12 months in a year.

2. What is the shortest month?
February has 28 or 29 days.

3. Which months have 31 days?
January, March, May, July, August, October, December.

4. How can kids remember months easily?
Using rhymes, knuckle tricks, and seasonal grouping.

5. Why is it important to learn the months?
It helps in planning, reading calendars, and organizing daily life.
