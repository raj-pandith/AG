Eligibility Notes

Admissions are based on the cut-off age criteria as per the table. Ensure documents are ready before applying.

Grade II to IX

Students seeking admission must submit a Transfer/School Leaving Certificate (Original) from the previous school, countersigned by an authorised signatory.

A photocopy of the Report Card of the grade passed is also required.

If the Transfer Certificate is not submitted during admission, it must be provided within 30 days of joining, failing which the student will forfeit admission.

General

Students will be enrolled on receipt of the duly filled admission application form along with the Wellness form.

The application form and supporting documents must be submitted at the school location or online.
