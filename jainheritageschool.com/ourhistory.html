Journey of Excellence

A year-wise timeline of growth, innovation, and impact.

1

1990 – 2000

  • Founded Sri Bhagawan Mahaveer Jain College, Bengaluru
  • Initiated Cataracted Free Kanakapura Project
  • Established Jain International Residential School (300+ acre campus)
  • A nation building excercise undertaken in the form of opportunity school Jain Vidyaniketan at kanakapura (Karnataka)
  • Human Networking Academy(HNA) gets established to enhance the capabilities of individuals and organization through professional training research and consultancy
2

2001 – 2005

  • Established MATS Institute of Management & Entrepreneurship
  • Initiated Mega Eye Camp with Project Drishti (2050 patients regained vision)
  • Jain Academy for Sporting Excellence the first sports school in India
  • Established Chenraj Roychand Center for Entrepreneurship
  • Jain Heritage School, a day-boarding school, with extended day care center gets established in Hebbal, Bengaluru
  • Launched Radio Active 90.4 MHz
2

2005 – 2010

  • Set up ARKA Eduserve Pvt. Ltd.
  • Jain (Deemed-to-be University) get established and operations commenced
  • 6 dedicated research centers set up
  • Signed MoU with TÜV Rheinland to offer (safety, energy programs)
  • MoU Signed with UL - USA to setup fire safety labs
3

2011 – 2015

  • 2A/S Coy 7 KAR Battalion NCC boys Bag the Best Institution Award
  • Jain (Deemed-to-be University) receives sanction from DRDO-DST for the development of a low cost reusable MICAV
  • Team Jatayu wins SAE Aero Design Competition, California
  • Dr. Chenraj Roychand receives the prestigious Kempegowda Award from the Government of Karnataka for his contribution to the field of education
  • Team Jatayu wins accolades at the SAE Aero Design Competition 2013held in Van Nuy California
  • Southern Champs, Jain (Deemed-to-be University) Bengaluru clinch the inaugural Toyota University Championship
3

2015 – 2017

  • Jain (Deemed-to-be University) awarded the ISO 9001:2008 certification by TÜV Rheinland
  • Athletes - Gagan Ullalmath & Sharath Gayakwad represents India at London Olympics & Paralympics
  • My Country Run movement to develop a sports as a career, and fitness as a way of life unveils
  • Jain University Incubation Center (JUIC), a joint initiative with DST, Govt. of India and ALSTOM France, is established to promote technology based enterprises by using new tools
  • Jain International Residential School ranks No. 1 in India among the top boarding schools by Education Today
4

2018 – Present

  • University Grants Commission awards Graded Autonomy Status to Jain (Deemed to be University)
  • Established ARKA Jain University, Jharkhand
  • Launch of Jain Online, the digital learning platform
  • Jain Heritage School introduced for the innovative learning environment for students
