We are a school that prides itself not only in fostering academic excellence but also in making our learners spend satisfying, fulfilling and joyous moments in school. These formative years should be remembered by our children throughout their lives.

🏫 Day Boarding School

At Jain Heritage School, our Day Boarding Program ensures a safe, structured, and engaging environment beyond regular school hours.

  • Extended Learning: Guided academic sessions with faculty support for homework and projects.
  • Balanced Routine: A blend of academics, recreation, and relaxation designed for holistic growth.
  • Nutritious Meals: Healthy and balanced food prepared in our in-house kitchen.
  • Support for Working Parents: Peace of mind knowing your child is in a secure, caring space.
“Education should be for life, not just for a living.”
🏸 After School Sports

Our After School Sports Program cultivates teamwork, discipline, and fitness through professional training in world-class facilities.

  • Sports Offered: Cricket, Football, Basketball, Tennis, Skating, and more.
  • Badminton Arena: Equipped with wooden flooring and professional-grade courts for safe play.
  • Expert Coaches: Certified trainers provide personalized attention and mentoring.
  • Holistic Fitness: Focus on endurance, coordination, and sportsmanship.

Our students regularly represent the school in inter-school and state-level tournaments.

🔬 STEAM Labs

Our STEAM Labs combine Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts, and Mathematics into hands-on, discovery-based learning experiences.

  • Innovation in Action: Robotics, 3D printing, and AI-driven projects.
  • Collaborative Learning: Encourages teamwork and critical thinking.
  • Creativity & Design: Integrating art and engineering for real-world solutions.
  • Future-Ready Skills: Prepares students for emerging careers and challenges.

Students explore, experiment, and innovate in a safe, stimulating environment.

🕒 Extended Day Care

The Extended Day Care facility provides a home-like environment within the school premises, ensuring your child’s comfort, safety, and joyful engagement after school hours.

  • Homework Assistance: Supervised study sessions led by trained staff.
  • Creative Play: Storytelling, art, and games for relaxation and fun.
  • Healthy Snacks: Nutritious evening meals included daily.
  • Safe Supervision: Monitored spaces with CCTV and trained caretakers.
🌟 Leadership Program

The Leadership Development Program cultivates confidence, empathy, and vision among students — preparing them to lead with purpose and integrity.

  • Student Council: Elected representatives learn teamwork and accountability.
  • Public Speaking Workshops: Enhancing communication and stage confidence.
  • Mentorship Sessions: Interaction with alumni and professionals.
  • Community Projects: Leadership through service and social impact.
🌍 International Exchange Program

Our International Exchange Program broadens students’ horizons through cross-cultural learning and collaboration with partner institutions worldwide.

  • Global Exposure: Exchange visits and joint learning modules with international schools.
  • Virtual Classrooms: Cross-border discussions and projects.
  • Cultural Immersion: Developing respect and empathy for global diversity.
  • Future-Ready Skills: Encouraging adaptability and international understanding.
🎯 Career Guidance Cell

The Career Guidance Cell empowers students to identify their strengths, explore career paths, and make informed academic choices.

  • Psychometric Assessments: Identify aptitudes and areas of interest.
  • Career Counselling: One-on-one sessions with certified experts.
  • University Tie-ups: Guidance on national and international admissions.
  • Skill Building: Workshops on resume writing, interviews, and communication.

Helping every child find their path, passion, and purpose.
